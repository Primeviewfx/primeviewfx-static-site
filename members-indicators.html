PrimeViewFX Indicators

Directional Boxes for MetaTrader 5 and TradingView. Each plots a series of stacked levels above or below price — when price closes confirmed through a level, it's statistically more likely to continue toward the next one, most reliably across the first two to three levels. Available to active subscribers.

MT5 · TradingView

Red (Bearish)

Four stacked levels above price. A confirmed close through a level signals a higher likelihood of continuing down toward the next one — watch for the close, not just a touch.

MT5 · TradingView

Green (Bullish)

Mirror of Red, plotted below price. A confirmed close through a level signals a higher likelihood of continuing up toward the next one — watch for the close, not just a touch.
