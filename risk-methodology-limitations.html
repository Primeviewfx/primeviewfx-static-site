Risk, Methodology & Limitations

PrimeViewFX is an educational and research dashboard for XAUUSD / Gold market structure.

Important notice

PrimeViewFX does not provide personal financial advice. PrimeViewFX does not tell users to buy, sell, enter, exit, hold, increase, reduce or copy any trade.

Users are responsible for their own analysis and decisions.

Not authorised by the FCA

PrimeViewFX is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

The dashboard is provided for educational and research purposes only. It should not be treated as personal investment advice, portfolio advice, trade execution instruction, or a recommendation tailored to any individual user.

High-risk products

Gold may be traded through products such as CFDs, spread bets, futures, options or leveraged derivatives. These products can be high risk. Losses can occur quickly. Leveraged products can magnify both gains and losses.

Only trade if you understand the product, the risk, and the possibility of losing money.

No guarantees

PrimeViewFX models are experimental. Past model performance does not guarantee future results.

  • A CORE level can fail.
  • A channel can break.
  • A box can be ignored.
  • A range-breaker can reverse.
  • A model-confirmed research condition can still lose.

No level, state, box, channel, range or model output is guaranteed.

Methodology summary

PrimeViewFX uses modelled structure layers including weekly EMA5 channel state, green/red range boxes, reconstructed ladder levels, CORE / SECONDARY / REJECT survival classifications, strict activation research rules and channel-state validation.

The dashboard converts these into research labels such as MODEL_CONFIRMED_RESEARCH, SCALP_ONLY, WATCH_ONLY, TRANSITION_ONLY, CONTEXT_ONLY and REJECT. These labels are not trading instructions.

Plain-English summary

PrimeViewFX sells the map, not the trade.

The dashboard helps users understand Gold structure. It does not make trading decisions for them.
